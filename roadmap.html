Get unstuck in tech.

The Practical 5-Step Roadmap to Accelerate Your Tech Career walks you through five exploratory steps towards an impactful, purpose-driven and rewarding career.

Free. No spam, just the guide and the occasional helpful note from Maria.

(01) — Inside the guideIdentify · Overcome · Initiate

01

Identify your zone of genius

Discover new growth and opportunities that align with who you are, so you do work that's meaningful to you.

02

Overcome your saboteur

Learn how saboteurs block your growth, and how to spot and manage them so you wake up motivated to start your day.

03

Initiate the move

Steer your tech career in the right direction and regain your self-determination and purpose.

(02) — What you'll gain

Tools for growth in your direction.

  • Wake up with a sense of purpose, motivated to start your work day
  • Feel more effective and focused, with work that's meaningful to you
  • Pursue new opportunities that align with who you are and what you want
  • Connect with the right people for your chosen career path
  • Regain a sense of pride in your achievements
  • Have the impact in your industry you know you're capable of

(03) — About the coach

I felt stuck too. Then I found it.

Feeling stuck and unmotivated in a career that didn't fit my strengths led me on a voyage of discovery to find what I'm truly passionate about. Now, as a qualified coach, I help tech professionals get unstuck and find a purpose-driven, rewarding career. After helping over 400 clients, I feel that fulfilment every day, and I want you to have it too.
