(02) — Leadership transformationsIn their words

M

Michelle Lamberta

Director of Customer Success · MaestroQA

IC Manager Director

"A log flume. A strenuous, steep climb where at the top I faced my biggest fears. Once I overcame them I could let go. Now I'm enjoying the ride, leading my team from a place of human connection and inner peace."
Read Michelle's story
Focus
Two pivotal moments at a tech startup: joining the customer success team, then managing and now directing it.
Growth
Confidence in her decisions, executive presence with the C-suite, and giving valuable feedback without fear, building a culture of trust and psychological safety.
What's different
More fulfilled at work, less anxious, more inner peace, and comfortable navigating ongoing challenges.
What enabled it
Letting go of perfectionism, leading with vulnerability, and dropping the belief that she had to climb the ladder to feel worthy. "There is no need to burn out anymore."

A

Ana Paula Mendez

Sr. Manager, Franchise Strategy & BI · The Coca-Cola Company

Stuck Clear Aligned

"Tu ayuda a ordenar el desorden."Your help to put order into the chaos.
Read Ana's story
Goals
More purpose and impact for herself and her team, and stronger relationships with her boss and direct reports.
Clarity
"I had no idea what I wanted and felt stuck. Now I'm very clear about what I want and need, and I can explain it." She applied for roles aligned with where she wants to grow.
Confidence
Clearer communication helped her cut through the noise, get to the root cause and create more impact in meetings and projects.
What helped
Tools to explore, accountability that gave her discipline, and Maria's first-day reminder: "You have to do the work."

L

Lauren Brady

Director of Digital Consulting · HigherVisibility

100% in 85 / 15 Bigger impact

"I trust and believe in my team, and that empowers them to own their decisions. A win-win."
Read Lauren's story
Goals
Accountability and ownership across the team: releasing control so people plan, decide and own their path to success.
Biggest shift
Letting go of controlling, an ingrained behaviour that had served her well but had become the biggest obstacle to her advancement.
What's different
She now spends 15% of her time working on the business and 85% in it, up from 100% in it, managing people and putting out fires.
What's possible
Contributing in more meaningful ways, a bigger impact on department and company growth, and honouring her boundaries.
