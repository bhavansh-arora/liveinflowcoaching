01

Option one

Career transitioning

Often unfulfilled by your day's work, doubting your job uses your zone of genius, or craving the inspiration to do something different? I get it. I've been there.

You'll

Clarify your next career move

Figure out if it's time for a new chapter, and understand what really matters to you so you can navigate the big decisions.

You'll

Turn dreams into achievable goals

Work out what you're meant to do and transition confidently into fulfilling work that matters to you.

We meet on Zoom, with video, in a safe space to dive deep into where you are, who you are and where you want to be. Each session is tailored so you reclaim your voice, gain self-confidence and trust your decisions. I'll be your cheerleader and accountability partner.

No challenge is too big and no dream is out of reach. Whether your next chapter is music producer, yoga teacher, life coach or founder of your own beer company, I can help you make the transition smoothly.

Turn your dreams into reality

02

Option two

Executive presence

Want to engage, align and inspire people to act? Executive presence is often the missing link between merit and success.

Leaders without executive presence often stall, despite having the experience and qualifications. Whether you're new or seasoned, we'll refine the skills that give you more influence and impact: awareness, curiosity, communication, accountability, inspiring action, valuing differences and a possibility mindset that shows the essence of your leadership brand.

I also work specifically with women in tech who want to reach their full leadership potential. Past projects include the Women in Tech programme with LinkedIn, High Performing Leaders with Twitch, Reddit's Key Talent Program, and work with HubSpot and G-Hub.

03

Option three

Become an entrepreneur

Started your business and unsure what to focus on first? Or ready to put your idea into action but unsure where to begin? I've been there.

Being an entrepreneur means wearing many hats, most of them new. After ten-plus years with big multinationals and building my own business, I've learned that we all take different routes to our goal. We'll find your unique way to make your business shine.

Together we'll set a solid foundation for your brand, whether it's a yoga studio, a travel consultancy, a gaming app or music production, identify strategies to build your online presence, and map the next step forward. Where it helps, I'll connect you with my own resources.

(03) — QuestionsBefore we begin

Good to know.

How do we get started?

Book a free 20-minute virtual coffee. We'll talk about where you are and what you want, and see if we're a good fit for your intentions.

Where do sessions happen?

On Zoom, with video, so you can work with Maria from Vancouver, across Canada and the US, or anywhere in the world.

Who do you usually work with?

Driven, hyper-achieving professionals, mostly in tech, who feel stuck, unmotivated or unchallenged, plus new and senior leaders building their presence and people starting a business.

Can we work in Spanish?

Sí. Maria is trilingual and has coached clients in Spanish as well as English.

Do you work with teams too?

Yes. Maria designs workshops and keynotes for groups from 5 to 300 people. See Workshops.
