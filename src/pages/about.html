(02) — My storyThe Queen of Transition
{{img street | Maria in a yellow blazer looking back over her shoulder in a whitewashed street | (min-width: 900px) 34vw, 100vw | | | 55% 30%}}

It looked like I had it all. Only a few people knew the truth.

After working in many countries and industries, I believed managing digital strategy was what I wanted. My teams and I worked with clients like Microsoft, Disney and Adidas. With a business degree, a master's in international business and a career climbing the ladder, it looked like success.

Inside, I felt miserable, exhausted and despondent. People called me the "Queen of Transition" because I'm good at navigating change, yet I had a deep fear of it and poor self-confidence. I was avoiding the very work that would give my life meaning.

Help from others finally made me notice my real needs. So I made a promise to myself and took a leap of faith. Yes, it was scary to quit a successful career and find my own path. But today I know what gives me meaning, and I'm doing what I'm meant to do.

"Life is too short not to live our dreams. Excitement lives on the other side of your comfort zone."

(03) — Now

I help people find work that means something.

As a Certified Professional Co-Active Coach and ICF Professional Certified Coach, I coach individuals and groups in multinational organisations, supporting ambitious professionals who feel stagnant, struggle with motivation and want fulfilment at work.

With me you'll be challenged to make some hard changes in how you see yourself. Together we'll uncover the work that gives you purpose, so you can find your flow and make the transition confidently and elegantly.
